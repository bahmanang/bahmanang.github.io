I am an Associate Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](http://www.math.miami.edu/) at the [University of Miami](http://www.miami.edu/). My research focuses on stochastic control and its applications to finance and insurance. In recent years, my work has centered on the theory of Predictable Forward Performance Processes (see [here](http://arxiv.org/abs/1611.04494), [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03608), and [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16228)) and on optimal consumption and investment problems with habit formation and loss aversion (see [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.02277), [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.03414), and [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.20063)). Additional information about my research can be found on my [publications page](https://bahmanang.github.io/publications/).

I received my DPhil in Mathematics from the [University of Oxford](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/) in 2014 under the supervision of [Thaleia Zariphopoulou](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/zariphop/). Further details are available on my [Mathematics Genealogy Project page](https://www.genealogy.math.ndsu.nodak.edu/id.php?id=184786) and in my [curriculum vitae](https://bahmanang.github.io/files/Angoshtari-CV.pdf).
